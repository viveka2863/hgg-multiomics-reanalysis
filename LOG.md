## 2026-10-20 PRE-REGISTRATION: C8 part B, does the co-activation rule beat chance and global shifts? (exploratory; written BEFORE running any part B script)
Provenance: the question arises from the C8 main results (TF-target layer passes 28/28 candidate edges; V1 accepts 26/28; the Q-C ratio assumes independent layers; IKAP infers kinase activity from phosphosites, which makes layers 1 and 2 dependent). - Q-H1 Label-permutation null: enumerate all 4,200 distinct assignments of the 10 samples to groups of sizes 3 (Cortex), 4 (PDGFRA), 3 (NTRK); for each, recompute the three layers' group means for the candidate edges and apply R0. Report accepted edges, paper nodes recovered, and the layer base rates (all kinases, phosphosites, TF target sets) after centring.
- Q-H3 Circularity (descriptive): the number of the 28 candidate edges whose TF site appears with that kinase in the KINASE column of hgg_phosphosite_matrix_normalized_whole_v1 (the matrix IKAP used).
