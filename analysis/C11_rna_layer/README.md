# C11 RNA layer (design pre-registered in LOG.md, 2026-10-21)

- **Kept:** the paper's integration of transcriptome, proteome and phosphoproteome; Supp Data 3 (18 RNA-seq samples in two batches) with the paper's ratio columns, and the ENCODE TF target sets as in C6.
- **Compared:** RNA vs protein vs best-site phospho log2 ratios (the paper's ratio columns and our rescaled all-10 protein); all-sample vs subset reproduction of the SD3 ratios; PCA and Ward clustering by group and batch; TF enrichment from RNA vs from protein (C6).
- **Added:** a joint subset search (supplement; gives the same subsets as the pre-registered sequential search), sign agreement, and the RNA-and-protein TF intersection.
- **Result:** SD3 has 21,943 rows (21,941 unique names). RNA vs protein Spearman: NTRK/Cortex 0.702 (0.707 with our rescaled all-10 protein), PDGFRA/Cortex 0.696 (0.697), NTRK/PDGFRA 0.649 (0.667); best-site phospho vs protein: 0.663 (0.659), 0.660 (0.661), 0.613 (0.598); sign agreement 0.97-1.00 among genes with |ratio| >= 1 in both layers. The SD3 ratio columns are reproduced with all 18 samples (median abs. difference about 3e-16 on the log2-of-means scale; the best subsets are the full groups, 4 Cortex / 7 PDGFRA / 7 NTRK). PCA: PC1 70.0%, PC2 20.8%; Ward k = 3 ARI 1.0 vs group; tumours only, k = 2: ARI 1.0 vs genotype and -0.08 vs batch. RNA TF enrichment: 9,960 DE genes in a 15,789-gene universe (63%); AUC 0.715; 5 enriched TFs (E2F4, RAD21, REST from the benchmark); protein (C6) AUC 0.726 with 4 enriched; enriched in both: E2F4 and REST (Jaccard 0.286; 2 of 23 benchmark TFs).
- **Verdict:** C11a concordant (Spearman >= 0.5 in 4 of 4 tumour-vs-cortex comparisons). C11b the RNA ratios use all samples (all 7 NTRK RNA samples), unlike the protein and phospho ratios (TRK1+TRK2, C2 part C). C11c the RNA data separate genotype and not batch (ARI 1.0 vs -0.08); TF activity from RNA recovers the benchmark about as well as protein (AUC 0.715 vs 0.726), but only 2 of the 23 TFs are enriched in both layers. Limits: the RNA samples cannot be mapped to the ten proteomic samples; the RNA DE rule is the protein rule applied to RNA (assumption); the RNA DE list covers 63% of its universe.
