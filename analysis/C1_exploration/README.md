Kept: the paper's Fig. 2 setup (PCA on log2 of all features; Ward clustering of z-scored top-1000-MAD log2 features) as the reference, on the whole proteome and phosphoproteome in the repo (RNA-seq is not in the repo; see C11).
Compared: 16 PCA + k-means variants and 64 hierarchical variants (log2 vs raw; all vs top-1000 features; centred vs scaled; Euclidean on z-scored features vs 1 - Pearson; ward.D, ward.D2, average, complete), cut at k = 3 (all samples) and k = 2 (tumours only); ARI against genotype.
Added: leave-one-sample-out stability; PD1-PD2 replicate check (median absolute log2 difference of all sample pairs).
Result: proteome paper setup reproduced (PC1 76.1%, PC2 12.0% vs paper 76/12; ARI 1.0 all samples and tumours only). Phospho PC1 67.7%, PC2 13.4% (paper 66/15) but the three-group recovery is not clean (ARI 0.676, PCA and ward.D; tumour-only hierarchical ARI 1.0, PCA 0.432). Only 22-38% of variants recover the groups; they are essentially log2 + top-1000 features. Leave-one-out stable (>= 0.8 in >= 9/10): hierarchical 6/10 in both layers; PCA 9/10 proteome, 3/10 phospho. PD1-PD2 are not the most similar within-group pair (rank 4 and 5 of 12): no evidence of technical replicates.
Verdict: method-dependent (pre-registered rules: Q-B fraction 0.22-0.38 < 0.9; Q-D fails). Failures are dominated by raw intensities and z-scoring all features (exploratory breakdown). Limits: n = 10; the repeated ARI values (0.676, 0.432) are the signature of one NTRK sample being grouped with PDGFRA; which sample is tested in part B; RNA layer not in repo.
