Kept: paper-style pipeline (one-way ANOVA across Cortex/PDGFRA/NTRK, 1000-permutation pooled-null p, BH < 0.05, max pairwise |log2FC| >= log2(1.5) proteins / log2(2) phospho) on hgg_whole_v1 and pho_hgg_uniq_v5 (original/c2_qa.R); the 4,703-protein list in HGG_whl_input as the benchmark.
Compared: parametric ANOVA, permutation ANOVA, limma F; FC none/1.5/2; FDR 0.05/0.01; with vs without the per-sample rescaling; with vs without TRK3 (improved/c2_main.R).
Added: NTRK-vs-PDGFRA limma t lists (all 10 and without TRK3), leave-one-sample-out stability, phospho normalised to gene-level protein.
Result: protein list not reproduced: 5,466 DE on raw data (Jaccard 0.549 vs the paper's 4,703), 4,927 after the authors' per-group rescaling (Jaccard 0.738, precision 0.83, recall 0.87). Phospho 6,758 vs 6,768 is a count only (any FC-2 variant gives 6,758-7,164). List size is set by the fold-change filter: without it about 11,000 of 13,567 proteins pass FDR 0.05; FC 1.5 gives about 5,500, FC 2 about 1,630. P-value method barely matters at paper thresholds (Jaccard 0.997 parametric, 0.985 limma; phospho 0.996, 0.943) but permutation ANOVA collapses at FDR 0.01 (389 vs 5,466 proteins). Rescaling changes the list (Jaccard 0.572). NTRK vs PDGFRA: 527 proteins and 751 phosphosites (59% and 56% higher in NTRK); 820 and 1,363 without TRK3, with 95% and 97% of the all-10 hits kept in the same direction (Jaccard 0.59, 0.53). 3-group list stable in 10/10 leave-one-out runs in both layers; genotype list 7/10 (protein), 3/10 (phospho). Phospho normalised to protein: 7,035 vs 4,226 DE, Jaccard 0.279.
Verdict: C2a (DE lists): robust to the test and to sample removal by the pre-registered rule, but the protein list is not reproduced from raw data, depends on the rescaling, and is effectively set by the fold-change cutoff. C2b (NTRK-vs-PDGFRA lists): method-dependent (Q-D fails; stability 7/10 and 3/10; mostly a power effect, hits retained in the same direction). C2c (normalised vs un-normalised makes no difference): not supported at the feature level (Jaccard 0.279); the paper's statement concerns pathway analysis (see C4). Limits: Q-B/D/E/F use the unscaled matrix; no phospho benchmark list (Supplementary Data 2 deferred).
