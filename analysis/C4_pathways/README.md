# C4 pathway enrichment (design pre-registered in LOG.md, 2026-10-19)

- **Kept:** the paper's idea of pathway over-representation on KEGG sets (JUMPn/JUMPnp/kegg_DB, 186 sets) with the authors' ortholog table; Q-A reruns it on the paper's WP-C1/WP-C5 labels (original/c4_main.R).
- **Compared:** ORA vs FCS (Wilcoxon) vs fgsea; own vs common background; DE list vs size-matched top-N; unnormalised vs protein-normalised phospho; BH 0.05 vs 0.2; ortholog table vs upper-cased symbols; leave-one-sample-out (improved/c4_variants.R, improved/c4_qe.R).
- **Added:** classification of 9 pre-registered cancer-signalling sets as phospho-only / proteome-only / both / neither in 8 settings and 10 leave-one-out runs; mapping coverage; Jaccard of enriched sets across normalisation and methods.
- **Result:** Q-A: KEGG_CELL_CYCLE enriched in WP-C1 (34/98, BH 4.4e-6) and 6/6 WP-C5 themes enriched in WP-C5. Q-B: 0 of 9 S_wide sets enriched in the phospho layer in any setting; claim holds in 0/8 variants and 0/10 leave-one-out runs; in the main setting no S_wide set is enriched in either layer (phospho DE genes are 43.4% of detected phospho genes, 2,354/5,421, and signalling sets sit at chance). Q-C: 0 enriched sets in either phospho list, Jaccard undefined, Spearman of -log10 p 0.60. Q-D: proteome Jaccard ORA-FCS 0.47, ORA-GSEA 0.32; phospho undefined.
- **Verdict:** C4a (proteome themes): supported. C4b (phospho-only cancer pathways): not supported under measured-universe backgrounds (pre-registered rule). C4c (normalisation at pathway level): inconclusive (no enriched sets). Proteome pathway lists are method-dependent. Limits: old KEGG collection lacks FoxO/HIF-1/PI3K/PKB sets; phospho clusters not in repo; only 35% of mapped gene-set genes fall in the phospho background; dependence on the reference set is tested in part B (exploratory).
