# C6 TF activity (design pre-registered in LOG.md, 2026-10-21)

- **Kept:** the paper's idea of testing ENCODE proximal TF target sets (JUMPn/JUMPnt/TF_DB, 115 TFs) for over-representation among the protein DE list, with the 23 TFs of Fig. 5b as the benchmark (original/c6_qa.R; own universe, anova_perm FC 1.5 FDR 0.05 list).
- **Compared:** three universes (own 11,717 / U1 16,795 / U3 7,568 genes), three DE lists (anova_perm; NTRK-vs-PDGFRA DE_all10 and DE_noTRK3), ORA vs FCS (Wilcoxon on -log10 limma F adj.P).
- **Added:** leave-one-sample-out stability and descriptive CRISPR ranks (n = 5).
- **Result:** Q-A: 4 TFs enriched (E2F4, REST, STAT2 from the benchmark; STAT1); AUC 0.726 vs size-only baseline 0.623 (margin 0.103); overlap p 0.0275; benchmark TFs have larger sets (median 139 vs 95.5 genes). Q-B: AUC 0.726 / 0.750 / 0.722 (own / U1 / U3) but enriched TFs 4 / 26 / 6 (Jaccard own-U1 0.15, own-U3 0.67). Q-C: NTRK-vs-PDGFRA lists AUC 0.512 (all 10; 484 genes) and 0.659 (without TRK3; 742 genes), 1-2 of 23 enriched, Jaccard with vs without TRK3 0.67. Q-D: ORA AUC 0.726 (4 enriched) vs FCS 0.784 (7 enriched), Jaccard 0.375. Q-E: AUC >= 0.7 in 8 of 10 runs (TRK1 0.694 and TRK2 0.671 fail; TRK3-dropped 0.758). Q-F: MYC rank 21 / 6 / 9 and JUN 37 / 53 / 64 vs non-hits JUND, EP300, BRCA1 at 43-84 (own / U1 / U3); in the own universe both hits outrank all non-hits (chance 1 in 10).
- **Verdict:** C6a (recovery of the paper's 23 TFs): supported by the pre-registered rule, thin margin (0.103; 3 of 23 enriched; partly a target-set-size effect). C6b (universe dependence): robust in ranking (AUC >= 0.7 under all universes), method-dependent in the enriched lists (4 / 26 / 6). C6c (genotype-level signal and stability): not supported (AUC <= 0.68) and not stable (8/10); the genotype lists are small, so power is limited. Limits: proteome only (no transcript layer); ENCODE targets are human cell-line ChIP-seq mapped to mouse (80% of target symbols have an ortholog); benchmark typed from Fig. 5b.
