# C9 CRISPR benchmark of the rankings (design pre-registered in LOG.md, 2026-10-21)

- **Kept:** the paper's CRISPR benchmark (Fig. 8b: 5 dropout hits Jun, Myc, Prkaa1, Prkaa2, Eef2k vs 4 non-hits Brca1, Ep300, Insr, Jund = B9); original arm = gene-level scores S1-S6 on B9 (`original/`).
- **Compared:** six gene-level scores (protein and phospho limma F and NTRK-vs-PDGFRA contrast; expression and phosphosite-count baselines) by AUC with an exact permutation p on B9 and the 15-gene B15; with and without TRK3 (`improved/`).
- **Added:** B15 (adds Cd93 hit and Cd74, Epha2, Spry1, Arhgap18, Dab2 non-hits), baselines, type-specific rankings (IKAP kinases K1, TF enrichment T1, network acceptance N1; descriptive), TRK3 arm.
- **Result:** B9 AUC S1-S6 = 0.69, 0.44, 0.55, 0.40, 0.44, 0.30 (exact p 0.24-0.87); B15 AUC 0.40, 0.27, 0.60, 0.36, 0.49, 0.49; minimum attainable p is 0.0079 (n = 9) or 0.0143 (n = 8, no Myc proteome); no score reaches AUC 0.8; expression baseline at chance. Without TRK3, S1-S4 AUC on B9 = 0.38, 0.31, 0.45, 0.30 (S1 falls from 0.69). Type-specific (descriptive; minimum p 0.10-0.25): IKAP F AUC 0.67, IKAP signed NTRK-PDGFRA effect AUC 0.0 (INSR, a non-hit, has the largest effect), TF enrichment AUC 1.0 (own universe), network acceptance AUC 1.0 (MYC 6 accepted edges vs 1, 1, 2).
- **Verdict:** no ranking validated by the pre-registered rule (AUC >= 0.8 and p < 0.05/6). The benchmark is too small and has no non-prioritised control set, so it cannot validate or refute the prioritisation. Limits: all nine regulators were pre-selected by the paper's pipeline; B15's extra genes may have been chosen for a strong NTRK-over-PDGFRA pattern (check the paper's Methods); labels typed from Fig. 8b and text; MYC standing out under the TF and network rankings is a descriptive lead only.
